# Benchmark — beating the best, honestly
_The founder's bar (2026-09-09): "beat every Python teaching course out there, especially
100 Days of Code." Know the competitor with respect; beat them in OUR lane._

## The competitor of record: 100 Days of Code (Dr. Angela Yu, Udemy)
Verified 2026-09-09: https://www.udemy.com/course/100-days-of-code/ — ~850,000+ students,
4.7-4.8★, one of the highest-rated courses in Udemy history; 100 unique projects in 100 days,
60+ hours, beginner → professional (web, automation, data science). Instructor ~3M students
across courses.

## What she does RIGHT (we steal these principles, shamelessly)
1. **A real project every single day.** Momentum through making, never through theory.
2. **Personality.** Warm, funny, human — students feel taught by a person, not a platform.
3. **Visible progression** — day numbers turn effort into identity ("I'm on Day 40").
4. **All-in-one promise** — never "now go buy the next course."

## The founder's joy principle (product law)
"I want them to enjoy, not to be challenged — the challenge might be part of the joy."
Translation into mechanics:
- Every lesson ends with something beautiful the child MADE (her project-a-day, our make-a-thing).
- Difficulty is invisible stairs, never a wall: worked example → tweak → make.
- Colors, motion, celebration copy — all tuned to be pleasing, never harsh. No red X anywhere.
- Failure states are warm and specific ("your turn looks like 72° — a star needs 144°").

## Honest gaps we must close over time (logged, not hidden)
- She has 100 projects; we have 12 lessons (Worlds 1-2). Depth comes World by World.
- She reaches web/data/automation; our Worlds 7-8 must genuinely get there for grade 11-12.
- She has personality on camera; our "voice" must come through writing and design (and later,
  optional teacher-led mode).
