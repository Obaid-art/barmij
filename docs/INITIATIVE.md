# Barmij برمج — the initiative (one page)
_Founded 2026-09-09. Working name: **Barmij** — the imperative "Code!", echoing the oldest
imperative in our tradition: اقرأ, "Read!". Alternate name: **Sifr** (صفر) — the Arabic zero that
gave the world the word "cipher"; we start every child from sifr. Founder decides._

## The intention (النية)
A free initiative — for the sake of Allah, not profit — so that **every student in the UAE
graduates genuinely competent in Python.** Built with the care of a scientist and gifted to the
Ministry of Education. Success metric: children who *make things*, not children who watched videos.

## Why now (verified 2026-09-09; see SOURCES.md)
- The Ministry merged AI + Computer Science into **one mandatory subject — "Artificial
  Intelligence and Technology" — across all three cycles** from this academic year (2026-27).
  Every school in the country now must teach this; the content layer is being furnished *now*.
- Existing national programs — One Million Arab Coders (2017), National Program for Coders
  (2021) — target **adults and the workforce** (video courses, golden visas, startups).
  The school-age, from-zero, visual-first, Arabic-soul layer is unfilled. That is our lane.

## The product principles (founder's words, engineering translation)
1. **"These are kids"** — intuitive, interactive, fun; "addictive in a good way": progress,
   streaks, and the real addiction — the gasp when your own loop draws a spiral. They build
   real things instead of grinding useless games.
2. **"Everything visual"** — every concept gets a beautiful figure; code itself is read through
   consistent **role colors** (the founder's manuscript design language: color = role, never
   decoration): blue = Python's words, lavender = names you invent, amber = values,
   rose = what happens (output/effects).
3. **"Extreme basics → very advanced"** — eight Worlds, from first `print` to real projects
   (docs/CURRICULUM.md). Real Python from minute one — no toy pseudo-language to unlearn.
4. **"Well established in pedagogy"** — PRIMM, constructionism, worked examples, misconception-
   driven design (docs/PEDAGOGY.md, with literature).
5. **No installation, nothing to break** — real Python runs in the browser (Pyodide/WebAssembly).
   One link works on any school computer, tablet, or home laptop.

## The path to the Ministry
1. Build Worlds 1-2 to a polish level that makes people stop talking.
2. Pilot with real kids in the family/school network; collect honest evidence it works.
3. Approach MoE with a working product + pilot data + a scientist's name on the pedagogy —
   offered free. (Channels: MoE innovation programs, Coders HQ community, ADEK/KHDA pilots.)

## Soft power (revised 2026-09-12: B23 characters KILLED by founder — no mascots, ever)
The export strategy stands without characters: English-language product (B29) with an
unmistakably Emirati soul → the Arab world first → global. That soul lives in its content
(falcons, zellij, Hope Probe, Eid, صفر and al-Khwarizmi taught as OUR history), its warmth
words, and its free-forever gift character — never in a mascot.

## What this is not — sealed (B24, 2026-09-14)
Not a business, not ad-funded, not data-hungry. A child's attention is an amanah (أمانة).
**A child never pays — no price, no premium tier, ever, in any country.** The founder weighed
charging and closed the question permanently, in his words: "I will stick for the sake of Allah,
and I will not take profit — not for this one. Allah will reward me with better things."
Sustainability, if ever needed, comes only from lanes that never touch a child's access:
institutional licenses, sponsorship/waqf endowment, grants — rules in DECISIONS.md B24.
