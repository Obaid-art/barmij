# Content Policy — originality & culture (law, not guideline)
_Founder decision 2026-09-09 (DECISIONS B15). Every lesson, project, example, dataset and
sentence in Barmij passes this document before it ships._

## 1. Originality — zero borrowed content
- Every project, exercise, example and code snippet is **authored by us, from scratch**.
  Nothing adapted from other courses (100 Days of Code included), textbooks, YouTube,
  Stack Overflow, or "classic exercises" collections.
- Inspiration from *pedagogy* (how to teach) is fine and cited in PEDAGOGY.md.
  Borrowing *content* (what is taught with) is not.
- Why: legal cleanliness, brand integrity, and the moat — content that is truly ours and truly
  UAE's cannot be undercut by any global course.

## 2. Cultural frame — UAE-souled, universally decent
Projects and examples come from the life our students actually live. Draw from:
- **Home & heritage:** the majlis, gahwa & dates, Eid, family (mother, father, siblings,
  grandparents — never romantic framings), hospitality.
- **The land & sea:** falcons, dhows, pearl diving, desert & ghaf trees, the Gulf.
- **The nation reaching up:** the Hope Probe and Mars, astronauts, National Day fireworks,
  the Union story, Expo-scale ambition.
- **Daily life:** football, gaming (making games, not gambling), karak, malls & souqs,
  the weather (a chart of Dubai in August teaches data AND humor), school itself.
- **Faith, naturally:** Ramadan moon phases (beautiful geometry), prayer times (real time-data),
  Eid gift budgets (arithmetic with joy). Respectful context, never preaching, never trivialized.

## 3. Banned framings (no exceptions, no discussion needed)
- Romance/dating examples ("my girlfriend/boyfriend…") — a family course, full stop.
- Alcohol, pork, gambling framings (dice and cards may teach probability as mathematics;
  betting, casinos and blackjack framings never appear).
- Imported holiday defaults (Halloween pumpkins etc.) — we have our own calendar to celebrate.
- Violence beyond cartoon-game abstraction; anything mocking any religion, nation, or dialect.
- Dark-pattern mechanics in ANY example (no simulated loot boxes, even as "just code").

## 4. Language & tone
- English content first; Arabic greetings and words woven in naturally (Ahlan, ya sadiqi,
  Mumtaz) — pride, not decoration. Full Arabic track: first-class, when built.
- Voice by tier: turtle-hero warmth for the young; maker/engineer respect for the old.
- Girls and boys equally present in every example set (names: Maryam, Khalid, Sultan, Sara,
  Hessa, Omar…). Emirati and expat-Arab and non-Arab names all appear — the real classroom.

## 5. Honesty (inherited DNA)
- Real-world numbers in datasets are either clearly playful/demo or sourced and dated.
- We never claim a project does more than it does ("this is a taste of machine learning,
  not the whole science").

## Review gate
New content ships only after founder (or a designated reviewer) checks it against this file.
When in doubt, ask: "Would a parent in the majlis smile at this example?" If unsure — rewrite.
