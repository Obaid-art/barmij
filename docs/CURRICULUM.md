## The three ranks (DECISIONS B14 revised) — earned, never age-gated
**Mastery, not birthdays.** Rank is computed from completed worlds; a prodigy of any age climbs
to the top. Typical ages below are a guide for parents/teachers only — the app never asks.

| Rank | Earned by | Typical age (guide only) | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| **مستكشف Mustakshif** (Explorer) | everyone starts here | often ~8–11 | Gentle pace, "Watch-then-try" everywhere, art-heavy, turtle-hero voice |
| **بنّاء Bannaa** (Builder) | completing World 1 | often ~12–15 | Standard experience, Worlds 2–6 |
| **رائد Ra'id** (Pioneer) | completing Worlds 1–2 (recalibrated as Worlds ship) | often ~16–18 | Engineer voice, applied Worlds 7–8, portfolio capstones |

Presentation comforts (bigger type, tap-to-insert tokens, compressed on-ramp for older
beginners) are **opt-in settings**, never age locks.

remix hint invites you to break it and make it yours. Tagged by rank, world, and concept; grown
in founder-reviewed batches under CONTENT_POLICY.md. First batch (~40) shipped 2026-09-09.

## The library ladder (DECISIONS B16 — scope discipline)
- **In:** turtle-canvas (ours), random, math → matplotlib (W7) → pandas-light (W7) →
  scikit-learn as a single honest capstone taste (W8, Ra'id only).
- **Out (deliberately):** web frameworks, Selenium/automation, deployment — vocational adult
  material, not school mastery. If a Ra'id graduate wants them, they'll learn them in a weekend —
  that ability IS our success metric.
- matplotlib is not "advanced": it draws. Continuity: forward/right → plt.plot. The child's
  fourth world of art makes charts feel like home.
