The guides of Barmij — concept board

Three style directions × two guides. Drafts for DIRECTION only — the final character deserves a professional illustrator and a trademark. React, choose, rename.
The idea on one line: a young Emirati boy and girl teach the world's children real Python — Doraemon carried Japan, Iftah Ya Simsim raised a region, our two carry the UAE. The child picks their guide; the turtle 🐢 becomes their beloved drawing pet.

Direction A — Geometric minimal

Pure rounded shapes, calm faces. Timeless, scales tiny (avatar) to huge (poster), easiest to animate.
The boy — white kandura + gahfiya cap
Waving hello. No ghutra — he's a kid, and kids wear the gahfiya.
The girl — teal mukhawar + gold headband, braids
Same geometry family, her own colors. Sister energy, equal billing.

Direction B — Storybook soft

Rounder cheeks, blush, softer shading — warmer, huggable, picture-book feel. More illustration work per pose.
The boy — softer, rounder, blush cheeks
One arm up mid-explanation — the natural teaching pose.
The girl — plum mukhawar, gold-tipped braids
Braid beads — the small true detail that makes her real.

Direction C — In-context: the guide replaces the 🐢 in lessons

How it feels inside a build beat — the guide's face speaks the captions.
print("Ahlan!")
print — Python's word. It means: say something.
Later: his recorded voice, Arabic and English — the modality principle (PEDAGOGY.md §8) was waiting for exactly this character.
